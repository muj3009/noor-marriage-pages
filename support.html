Account and login help

Noor Marriage supports phone verification and Google sign-in. If you cannot receive an OTP, check your phone number format, network connection, and try again. If the issue continues, email support.

Subscription help

Noor Premium Messaging unlocks messaging with matches. Purchases and renewals are managed through your app store account. To cancel or manage a subscription, open your device subscription settings and select Noor Marriage.

Safety

You can report or block a member inside the app. Safety actions such as block, report, and account support are free.

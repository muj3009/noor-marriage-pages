Privacy Policy

Noor Marriage

Effective date: 15 September 2026

Noor Marriage is a respectful Muslim marriage app designed to help members find meaningful matches, communicate safely, and manage conversations with clear intentions.

Information we collect

Depending on how you use Noor Marriage, we may collect:

  • Contact information: name, email address, and phone number.
  • Location information: approximate or coarse location, such as city or region, to help show relevant matches.
  • Profile and sensitive information: information you choose to add to your profile, such as religion, preferences, interests, and other details used for matching.
  • User content: photos, videos, messages, search history, customer support messages, reports, and other content you provide in the app.
  • Identifiers: user ID, device ID, account ID, and similar identifiers needed to operate the service.
  • Purchases: subscription status and purchase history. Payments are processed by Apple or Google; Noor Marriage does not receive your full payment card details.
  • Usage and diagnostics: product interaction, crash data, performance data, and similar technical information.

How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts, authenticate users, provide matching and messaging features, process subscriptions, prevent fraud and abuse, improve reliability, respond to support requests, and moderate safety reports.

We do not use your information for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

Sharing of information

We may share limited information with service providers that help us operate Noor Marriage, such as hosting, analytics, authentication, payment processing, crash reporting, and moderation tools. These providers may only use information to provide services to Noor Marriage.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect users, or to enforce our terms and safety rules.

Subscriptions and payments

Noor Premium Messaging is handled through the app store payment system. Subscription management, cancellation, refunds, and billing are controlled by the app store account used for purchase.

Data retention

We keep information for as long as needed to provide the app, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and protect the safety of members. You can request deletion of your account or personal data by contacting us.

Your choices

You can update profile information in the app, block or report members, manage subscription settings through your app store account, and request access or deletion of your data.

Children

Noor Marriage is intended for adults seeking marriage. It is not intended for children.

Security

We use technical and organisational safeguards to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security, but we work to protect user data and respond to issues promptly.

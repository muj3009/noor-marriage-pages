Child Safety Standards

Noor Marriage

Effective date: 15 September 2026

Noor Marriage is intended for adults seeking marriage. We do not permit child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sexual solicitation of minors, trafficking, sextortion, or any behaviour that harms or exploits children.

Our standards against CSAE

  • Users must not upload, request, create, share, promote, or distribute CSAM or any sexual content involving minors.
  • Users must not groom, coerce, extort, threaten, or attempt to sexually exploit a child.
  • Users must not use Noor Marriage to contact minors for romantic, sexual, exploitative, or unsafe purposes.
  • Users must not post content that sexualises children, normalises child exploitation, or directs users to CSAM or CSAE content elsewhere.
  • Accounts involved in CSAE or CSAM are subject to immediate restriction, removal, and reporting where required by law.

Reporting child-safety concerns in the app

Noor Marriage provides in-app reporting tools. Users can report a member from the member profile or safety/report flow. Reports may include inappropriate content, harassment, fake profiles, spam, offensive behaviour, or other safety concerns. Users can also block members.

If a report involves child safety, CSAE, CSAM, grooming, or exploitation, users should report it in the app and may also contact us directly at muj2019@gmail.com.

How we handle reports

We review safety reports and may remove content, restrict accounts, block access, preserve relevant information, or take other protective action. Where we become aware of apparent child sexual abuse material or exploitation, we will take appropriate action, including reporting to relevant regional or national authorities when required by applicable law.

Child-safety laws and cooperation

Noor Marriage is committed to complying with applicable child-safety laws and cooperating with lawful requests from relevant authorities. We do not tolerate attempts to use the service to exploit or endanger children.

Review and updates

We may update these standards as our safety practices, legal obligations, or platform requirements change.
